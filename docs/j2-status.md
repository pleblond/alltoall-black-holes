J₂ is adopted as the program's **canonical working vacuum substrate**.

This is an evidence-based working choice, not a uniqueness claim. The current dynamics has not derived J₂ from arbitrary primordial graphs, and no claim is made that the microscopic vacuum must be exactly J₂. A future substrate may replace it while preserving the phenomenology banked here.

## What J₂ has survived

**Geometry / coarse structure**

- Exact square quotient verified computationally.
- Every quotient edge is square-adjacent globally.
- Strict-interior quotient cells have exactly four square neighbours.
- Quotient shells are exactly \(4r\).
- Microscopic and quotient long-scale exponent agree: measured \(\Delta p=0\).
- J₂ retains substantial UV structure invisible in the square quotient: distinct shell/cut laws, return amplitudes and high \(C_4\) density.
- Isotropy program found no persistent \(h_1/h_2\) orientation selected by D5∞ formation.
- D5∞ formation transfers to the J₂ torus despite the pristine substrate being triangle-free.

**Locality / perturbation**

- J₂ survives the characterized perturbation probes without exhibiting an anomalous discovery-only tier.
- Deletion perturbations leave the measured long-scale exponent essentially unchanged.
- Swap damage gives family-typical response rather than a special protected tier.
