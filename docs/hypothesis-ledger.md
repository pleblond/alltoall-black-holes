# Hypothesis Ledger

**Purpose.** This ledger is kept **separate from the earned-verdict ledger**
(`docs/model.md`, `docs/scaffolding-history.md`, `docs/DEFERRED.md`, campaign
verdicts), so promising interpretations are never confused with established
results. Each entry below states an idea plus its hypothesized mechanism,
formula, constant, or value — short by design. Promotion to the earned record
requires a preregistered campaign plus a merged verdict, never a rewording here.

**Status vocabulary.**

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Derived** | substantially supported / earned already |
| **Derivable soon** | a concrete campaign or calculation can test it now |
| **Intuition** | conceptual hypothesis, not yet earned |

Refinements used below (`Derived / partial`, `Derived as debt`,
`Derived negative`, `Partial`, `Open`, `Future derivable`) qualify the three
words above; they do not promote anything to the earned ledger by themselves.

### H1. Dark-energy phenomenology from weave evolution (Intuition)

**Idea.** Evolving weave statistics produce a time-dependent effective
metric/transport scale. Cosmic expansion is then effective geometry from
weave evolution rather than a separate vacuum-energy component. This is worth
taking seriously as phenomenology — but must confront expansion data directly.

**Mechanism.** Introduce a cosmological scale factor for the weave, \(a(t)\),
and ask whether it derives from the weave statistics:

\[
\rho_S(t),\qquad \rho_I(t),\qquad M_{ab}(t),\qquad \ell_{\rm hit}(t),
\]

where \(\rho_S\) = sheet density, \(\rho_I\) = intersection density,
\(M_{ab}=\langle n_an_b\rangle\) = sheet-orientation matrix,
\(\ell_{\rm hit}\) = characteristic macroscopic spacing.

**Simplest candidate.** If spacing is controlled by sheet density,
\(\ell_{\rm hit}\propto\rho_S^{-1}\), then:

\[
\boxed{a(t)\propto\ell_{\rm hit}(t)\propto\frac{1}{\rho_S(t)}.}
\]

Immediately:

\[
\boxed{H(t)\equiv\frac{\dot a}{a}=-\frac{\dot\rho_S}{\rho_S}.}
\]

So cosmic expansion would literally mean \(\boxed{\dot\rho_S<0}\) — not
necessarily sheets disappearing; total sheet content could grow while sheet
area per emergent 3D volume decreases.

**Alternative ruler.** If intersections set the effective ruler instead:

\[
a\propto\rho_I^{-1/3},\qquad H=-\frac13\frac{\dot\rho_I}{\rho_I},
\]

depending on how \(\rho_I\) is defined. WEAVE should derive the exponent
rather than us choosing it.

**Dimension formation ≠ expansion.** The weave may first reach the 3D phase
(\(d_{\rm eff}\to3\)), then keep diluting (\(\rho_S(t)\downarrow\)) while
staying 3D:

\[
\boxed{\text{dimension formation}\neq\text{cosmic expansion}.}
\]

Early universe: \(d_{\rm eff}(t)\) evolving. Late universe:
\(d_{\rm eff}\simeq3\) with \(a(t)\) evolving. Today's expansion need not mean
\(3\to4\).

**Isotropy constraint.** If \(M_{ab}(t)=\langle n_an_b\rangle\to\frac13\delta_{ab}\),
the weave becomes isotropic; unequal eigenvalue evolution would give
direction-dependent expansion. The late-time weave must satisfy approximately:

\[
\boxed{M_{ab}\simeq\frac13\delta_{ab}}
\]

and preserve it extremely well. Cosmological isotropy becomes a constraint on
sheet-orientation statistics.

**Confrontation with data.** The observational object is the measured expansion
history \(H(z)\) and distances derived from it (BAO, supernovae, CMB). WEAVE
cosmology should predict something like:

\[
\boxed{\frac{H(z)}{H_0}=-\frac{1}{H_0}\frac{\dot\rho_S}{\rho_S}}
\]

(or whatever relation WEAVE actually derives), compared directly against
distance data — without first introducing \(\Omega_\Lambda\) or an
equation-of-state \(w\). Only afterward translate into an effective \(w(z)\)
for comparison with standard cosmology. Current DESI analyses test both
constant-\(w\) and \(w(a)=w_0+w_a(1-a)\); hints for evolving dark energy have
shifted between releases, so the methodologically clean move is to derive
\(\rho_S(t),\rho_I(t),M_{ab}(t)\) from the model and compare blind.

**Eventual observational targets.**

1. Distance–redshift / \(H(z)\) (BAO, supernovae).
2. Near-isotropy: \(M_{ab}\) evolution must not produce excess directional expansion.
3. Structure growth: changing weave density alters propagation/gravitational response.
4. Early-universe constraints: \(\rho_S(t)\) near recombination must respect CMB observables.
5. Stable local physics: ideally \(\rho_S(t)\) changes the macroscopic metric scale
   while dimensionless local physics stays fixed — the analogue of expansion
   without atoms themselves expanding.

**Desired separation.**

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{rcl}
\text{local J2 physics}&\approx&\text{constant},\\
d_{\rm eff}&\approx&3,\\
\rho_S,\rho_I&=&\text{slowly evolving},\\
M_{ab}&\approx&\frac13I,\\
a(t)&=&F(\rho_S,\rho_I).
\end{array}}
\]

**What would promote it.** Derive \(\rho_S(t),\rho_I(t),M_{ab}(t)\) from the
model, compute the implied \(H(z)\), and compare blind against BAO/SN/CMB
distance data.

### H2. Split outcome stochasticity — decay's two stochastic pieces (OPEN)

**Correction to the ledger.** "Decay products deterministic" was too strong.
The earned statement is: **deterministic if the relevant \(\xi\) is already
stored in \(Q\).**

**In the reduced state, split is underdetermined.** From \(M=(G,\psi)\) alone,
SPLIT-0 found a whole inverse fiber \(\boxed{\xi=(c,d)}\), and FIBER-0 showed
existing physics does not uniquely determine a measure over it
(\(\boxed{\text{FIBER0-DEBT}}\)). So \(M\to X_\xi\) has multiple possible
outcomes — calling that stochastic is reasonable if nature must choose a new
\(\xi\).

**STORE-0 changed the picture.** With the complete state \(\boxed{(G,\psi,Q)}\)
retaining the \(\xi\) lost during merge, \((M,Q=\xi)\to X_\xi\) is unique:
\(\boxed{\text{split is deterministic conditional on }Q.}\)

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{Known state} & \text{Split}\\
\hline
(G,\psi) & \text{underdetermined}\\
(G,\psi,Q) & \text{deterministic}
\end{array}}
\]

**Not established.** Physical decay need not replay a previously stored merge
entry. A spontaneous split may require creation/selection of a new \(\xi\),
in which case FIBER0-DEBT returns (\(\boxed{\text{new split}\Rightarrow\text{which }\xi?}\))
and products could be stochastic. Only if every physical split consumes an
existing \(Q\) entry are products predetermined with just timing stochastic.

\[
\boxed{\text{Decay may contain TWO potential stochastic pieces:}}
\qquad
\boxed{\underbrace{\text{when to split}}_{\text{timing}} + \underbrace{\text{which }\xi}_{\text{products, unless supplied by }Q}.}
\]

JET-0/TIME-Q bear on the first piece. Removing the second requires
establishing whether a physical decay-like split necessarily uses pre-existing
\(Q\).

**What would promote it.** Show decay-like splits consume (vs. create) \(Q\)
entries; derive/test the waiting-time law and any \(\xi\)-selection law
instead of assuming them.

**Status: not FAILED — leave open as we test it.** BH-ENT-0 predates the full
significance of \(\boxed{\mathcal X_{\rm full}=(G,\psi,Q)}\); its headline
counting (graph preimages + field blindness) does not close the post-STORE
question.

**Idea.** The natural post-STORE entropy census is no longer
\(\#\{\text{interior graphs}\}\) but:

\[
\boxed{\#/\mathrm{Vol}\{Q:(G,\psi,Q)\mapsto\text{same exterior observables}\}.}
\]

\((G,\psi,Q_1)\) and \((G,\psi,Q_2)\) can share identical reduced \((G,\psi)\)
while differing microscopically — many microstates → same exterior state, the
required conceptual object.

**The factor 2.** The generic continuous inverse fiber has \(d_{\rm cont}=2\),
precisely the complex relative mode \(\boxed{d=d_R+i\,d_I\in\mathbb C\simeq\mathbb R^2}\)
per store entry (plus discrete cover \(c\)). STORE says merge moves it into
\(Q\): each generic merge transfers 2 continuous real DOF into \(Q\), so
schematically \(\boxed{D_Q=2N_{\rm merge}+D_c}\). If independent stored modes
scale with boundary area (\(N_Q\propto|\partial R|\)), then
\(\boxed{\dim_{\mathbb R}Q_{\rm cont}\propto2|\partial R|}\) — the area-scaling
information dimension BH-ENT-0's raw census would have missed.

**Dimension, not entropy yet.** Continuous \((d_R,d_I)\) needs a measure,
resolution, or quantization before it yields entropy. Schematically, \(k\)
states per real DOF would give \(\Omega_Q\sim k^{2N_Q}\),
\(S_Q\sim\boxed{2N_Q\log k}\propto A\) if \(N_Q\propto A\) — a genuine area-law
mechanism — but we have no \(k\). Do not force the 2 into the BH \(1/4\); the
primitive question is whether
\(\boxed{\dim Q_{\rm exterior\text{-}blind}/|\partial R|\to\text{constant}}\)
with the 2 from complex \(d\).

**Test (BH-Q-ENT-0 — Boundary Scaling of Hidden Store Information).** Primary
observable \(\boxed{D_Q(R)=\dim_{\mathbb R}\{Q\text{ variations exterior-blind for }R\}}\);
test \(D_Q(R)\sim A\) vs \(V\) vs \(A\log A\) vs other, decomposed as
\(\boxed{D_Q=\underbrace{2N_d}_{\text{complex modes}}+\underbrace{D_c}_{\text{discrete cover}}}\).

**What would promote it.** Area-scaling \(D_Q(R)\) with the 2 from complex
\(d\); a physical measure/quantization turning dimension into entropy.
