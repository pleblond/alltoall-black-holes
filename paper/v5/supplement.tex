\section{Quantum-information methods (A--O, Q--Y)}

Log law $t_*(N) \approx \log_2 N/\log_2(1+p)$; MaxEnt $k \ge N s_{\mathrm{node}}/
s_{\mathrm{leg}}$ plus fixed point $k^*(N) = 16\pi(\varepsilon N/l_p)^2\allowbreak/4\ln 2$
(stable; BS gives $\varepsilon = c/\sqrt{N}$ and $\alpha = k^*/N = 1$ exactly);
QES crossing iff $s_{\mathrm{leg}} > l_p^2/4$ (Sec.~3 dies otherwise); Page
wiring-only vs $N$-shrinks identical $A(t)$; QEC $99\%$ at
$k \ge N/2+1+\log_2 100$; Kerr $k_{\mathrm{eff}} = A/l_p^2$ monotonic in spin;
exact Page $H(mn)-H(n)-(m-1)/2n$ with $0.72$-bit dip; CKW verified; OTOC
$\lambda$ + MSS $0.50$--$0.68$ ($\alpha \in [9.0,12.4]$, $11.24$ inside);
TN $S_{\mathrm{bdy}} = \min(N\log d, k\log D)$ fixing
$\varepsilon \approx 0.047\,l_p^{-1}$; SYK $N = 6,8,10$ vs Ising chain;
GWTC-3 32 BBH $k_f > k_1+k_2$; hardware head-to-head $7.9$ vs $14.6$ steps.
Figs.~7--20, 23--25, 30--33. Healing $dA/dt = (4\ln 2\,k\,l_p^2-A)/\tau$,
$\tau = 11.24M$ ($3.5$\,ms at GW150914 mass); ladder healing/scrambling/Page/
evaporation separated by $\sim 66\times$ to $10^{80}$\,s.

\textbf{What Page vs.\ island means here (honesty note).} The Page curve is derived as
random-state behaviour: idealised leg surgery $k\to k-1$ is imposed and $S_{\rm rad}=\min(t,N_{\rm eff}-t)$
holds by construction (module \texttt{evaporation}), but the qubit toy now has an explicit unitary
completion (module \path{evaporation_unitary}, Fig.~\ref{fig:sup-upage}): per-step $V_t$ with
$V_t^\dagger V_t=I$ verified, $S_{\rm rad}$ computed from $\rho_{\rm rad}$ tracks the finite-dimensional
Haar/Page expectation (mean dev.\ $0.002$ bits, $N=8$), and finite-depth all:all circuits converge to
Page by depth $\sim5$/step without assuming Haar. The QES/island crossing
$S_{\rm no}=ks_{\rm leg}$ vs $S_{\rm isl}=k\ln2+\max(S_0-ks_{\rm leg},0)$ is a two-saddle
competition plus a discrete min-cut analogue (module \texttt{qes}), not an extremization of
$S_{\rm gen}=A/4G+S_{\rm matter}$ from a gravitational path integral. The remaining missing theorem is the
graph-dynamics evaporation isometry $V_k:{\cal H}_{{\rm graph},k}\to{\cal H}_{{\rm graph},k-1}\otimes{\cal H}_{\rm leg}$:
derive (not merely allow) a scrambling $V_k$ from the graph Hamiltonian/adjacency.
Tracked as D1 in \path{docs/DEFERRED.md}.

\textbf{What Kerr means here (honesty note).} Only the Kerr--Newman area $A(M,a,Q)$ is assumed to
set the leg budget $k_{\rm eff}=A/4\ln2$ (module \texttt{kerr}; extremal Kerr half, extremal RN
quarter; ratios patch-independent). Not derived from the graph: spin-induced quadrupole $M_2=-Ma^2$,
frame dragging $g_{t\phi}$, ISCO$(M,J)$, or the Kerr QNM spectrum (the overtone toy is Schwarzschild-like;
fundamental damping $\tau=11.24\,M$ calibrated, footprint corrections $1/2k$, $1/\sqrt{k}$ unobservable).
Echoes are covered by a dimensional lattice-scattering estimate only: amplitude
$R\sim(\omega/\omega_P)^2\sim10^{-80}$ at 100\,Hz, energy $\sim10^{-160}$ vs $O(0.01)$
detectability ($\sim158$ orders of margin, module \texttt{gwdata}) --- not a leg S-matrix derivation.
The exterior perturbation problem ($\delta\omega_{nlm}$ vs Kerr) is open.
Hence GW250114 (SNR 80 area law + Kerr ringdown) and GW241011 (strongest $\delta\kappa$ bound to date,
Kerr to factor $\sim$2 resp.\ $\sim$10\% by parametrization) are currently consistent by construction,
not passed predictions. GW250114 provides the QNM benchmark ($\delta f_{220}\sim2\%$,
$\delta\tau_{220}\sim10\%$, $\delta f_{221}\sim30\%$, $\delta f_{440}\sim$tens\%):
a future graph-derived $|\delta\omega/\omega|$ above those thresholds fails.
A future graph derivation $Q=-Ma^2(1+\delta_Q)$ with $|\delta_Q|\gtrsim0.17$
would be ruled out by GW241011. Tracked as D2 in \path{docs/DEFERRED.md}.

\textbf{Kerr thermodynamics (conditional check).} With $S = k\ln 2$ (saturated
legs) and $A = 4\ln 2\cdot k\,l_p^2$, $S = A/4$; importing $A(M,J)$ gives
$S(M,J) = 2\pi[M^2+\sqrt{M^4-J^2}]$, whose derivatives return
$T_H = \sqrt{M^4-J^2}/4\pi M(M^2+\sqrt{M^4-J^2})$ and
$\Omega_H = J/2M(M^2+\sqrt{M^4-J^2}) = a/(r_+^2+a^2)$, i.e.\
$dM = T\,dS + \Omega_H\,dJ$ (module \texttt{thermo}: identities $< 10^{-6}$,
independent finite-step $\Delta M = T\Delta S + \Omega_H\Delta J$ to $O(d^2)$;
super-extremal $|J| > M^2$ returns NaN, never clamped). Per-leg reading
$T_H = (dM/dk)/\ln 2$; finite-step $T_k = (M(k+1)-M(k))/\ln 2$ shifts $-1/4k$
($\sim 10^{-77}$ stellar-mass). Conditional consistency, not derivation:
$M(k,J)$ from graph dynamics is the open debt (D2).
