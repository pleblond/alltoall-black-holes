\section{Gravity methods (AS, AT, AU, BB, BH, BI, BJ, BV)}

\textbf{AS entropic Newton.} Leg screens + equipartition + Bekenstein bound give
$F = M_1M_2/r^2$ exact (slope $-2$ to $10^{-9}$); leapfrog orbits close,
$T^2 \propto r^3$. Flux $\propto 1/r^2$ distinguished from force (temperature
corrects). Fig.~43.

\textbf{AT redshift.} GPS $+5.29\times 10^{-10}$ and Pound--Rebka
$2.55\times 10^{-15}$ derived with no new parameters. Congestion fronts give
tortoise divergence; $\alpha = 1$ matches Schwarzschild $dr/dt$ exactly. Fig.~44.

\textbf{AU Einstein--Hilbert routes.} Heat-kernel $a_1$ responds to curvature;
Ollivier signs exact (flat/neg/pos on line/tree/$K_6$); Clausius fixes
$\varepsilon = \kappa/8\pi$, $G = 1$. Raychaudhuri-for-leg-bundles open. Fig.~45.

\textbf{BB lensing + Shapiro + chroma.} Fermat $n = 1/c_{\mathrm{eff}}$ gives full
$4M/b$ (naive $2M/b$ bet lost and recorded). Shapiro log matches GR to 5\%.
Chromatic extension uses \emph{phase} velocity $1/24$, not group $1/8$
(conflating the two triples the prediction): $r$-independent factor drops out
of Born, leaving $10^{-56}$--$10^{-32}$. Figs.~49, 55.

\textbf{BH strain.} Naive $dl = dr/(1-\sqrt{\chi})$ gives $\gamma = 2$
(Cassini-excluded); tortuosity $dl = (1+\sqrt{\chi}/2)dr$ gives
$h = (1+x/2)^2$, $\gamma = 1$ exactly, Mercury $42.99''$/cy. Peel-off
$-0.75\,M/a$ measured. PPN: $\gamma = 1$ derived, $\beta$ unresolved
(coordinate-confused, not quoted), $\alpha_{1,2},\xi$, Nordtvedt untouched.
Figs.~58, 60.

\textbf{BI/BQ weak-field OR.} Grid+hub with flux stubs: radial OR negative in
all configs (sign derived). Direct mode $p = 0.92 \pm 0.48$ ($L = 9$); $1/r$
collapse beats $1/r^2$ (CV $0.47$ vs $0.60$). Green-function walk fails by leg
shorting ($h$-steepness $9.2 \to 4.1$); AS temperature triply load-bearing.
Figs.~59, 64.

\textbf{BV UV tortuosity-as-scattering.} Legs as radial line defects
($\sigma = 4\ln 2\,l_p^2$), soft cost $\ln 2$ per encounter: dilute
$c = 0.44$--$0.60$ (target $0.456$, geometric $1/\sqrt{\pi} = 0.564$) with zero
tuning; $p = 2c$, $\gamma = 2c$. Mixed mode ($0.75\,r_e$ core) rises then
disconnects at $k_{\mathrm{crit}}$ --- the pop without metric input. Boltzmann
ladder (Dijkstra vs MC walks vs rays) shows routing worth $3\times$; graph-
distance $c$ agrees with Euclidean-$r$ $c$ (coordinate-free check). Figs.~70--72.

\section{Quantum-information methods (A--O, Q--Y)}

Log law $t_*(N) \approx \log_2 N/\log_2(1+p)$; MaxEnt $k \ge N s_{\mathrm{node}}/
s_{\mathrm{leg}}$ plus fixed point $k^*(N) = 16\pi(\varepsilon N/l_p)^2/4\ln 2$
(stable; BS: $\varepsilon = c/\sqrt{N}$, $\alpha = k^*/N = 1$ exactly); QES
crossing iff $s_{\mathrm{leg}} > l_p^2/4$ (Sec.~3 dies otherwise); Page
wiring-only vs $N$-shrinks identical $A(t)$; QEC $99\%$ at
$k \ge N/2+1+\log_2 100$; Kerr $k_{\mathrm{eff}} = A/l_p^2$ monotonic in spin;
exact Page $H(mn)-H(n)-(m-1)/2n$ with $0.72$-bit dip; CKW verified; OTOC
$\lambda$ + MSS $0.50$--$0.68$ ($\alpha \in [9.0,12.4]$, $11.24$ inside);
TN $S_{\mathrm{bdy}} = \min(N\log d, k\log D)$ fixing
$\varepsilon \approx 0.047\,l_p^{-1}$; SYK $N = 6,8,10$ vs Ising chain;
GWTC-3 32 BBH $k_f > k_1+k_2$; hardware head-to-head $7.9$ vs $14.6$ steps.
Figs.~7--20, 23--25, 30--33. Healing $dA/dt = (4\ln 2\,k\,l_p^2-A)/\tau$,
$\tau = 11.24M$ ($3.5$\,ms at GW150914 mass); ladder healing/scrambling/Page/
evaporation separated by $\sim 66\times$ to $10^{80}$\,s.

\section{Compact objects and 2PN (BU)}

\textbf{Gradient shells.} $p_{\mathrm{adj}}(s) = 0.85+0.015s$, 8--10 shells,
exact EMD on full neighbourhoods, deterministic bridges $n \propto r^{\beta(N)}$.
Measured: $N = 1020$ ($10\times 102$), $\beta = 1.24$, 80 graphs exact Floyd+LP
($\sim 545$\,s): $p = 0.913 \pm 0.049$ per graph, SEM $0.0055$, stacked $0.911$
($R^2 = 0.956$), 80/80 fits ok, range $0.79$--$1.05$. Distance to target
$|0.913-0.92| = 0.007$ ($0.24\sigma$ in $p$, $\sim 0.09\sigma$ in $\dot\omega$),
$5\times$ precision margin ($0.0055$ vs $0.028$). Flat control $p = 0.49$ is
$-10\sigma$ dead.

\textbf{N-scale campaigns (BV).} CSR-direct shell graphs + Sinkhorn OR (no
NetworkX): N=1020 reproduced ($0.9107$ vs $0.9134$); N=4000 $p = 0.9315 \pm
0.0032$ ($\beta = 0.99$); N=8000 $p = 0.9382 \pm 0.0030$ ($\beta = 0.87$,
pods); N=16000 $p = 0.9137 \pm 0.0022$ ($\beta = 0.74$, GPU farm). Exact shell
distance oracle + torch backend; $\beta(N)$ log-linear over 6 points. Local-$p$
turnover $0.63$--$0.69 \to 1.26$--$1.46$ measured (inner windows flatten --- the
UV turnover). Kill wire: $p$ outside $0.92 \pm 0.056$ at $N = 1024$ class fails.

\textbf{2PN and pulsars.} $h = (1+x/2)^2$ gives $c_1 = 3.36$ vs GR $1.94$
(73\% excess); naive fixed-$M$ gives B1913 $6.2\sigma$, J0737 $9.7\sigma$ vs
real TOAs. With $c_2 = p(2p-1)$, $w = 1.953$:
$c_{\mathrm{tot}} = 4.8693$ at $p = 0.92$ vs GR $4.8695$ ($0.00\sigma$
fixed-$M$). Self-consistent $R+\dot\omega \to M$: $\Delta M = -11.2$\,ppm
naive ($-4.5$\,ppm with GR $g_{rr}$), $\Delta\sin i = 3.7\times 10^{-6}$,
$s_{\mathrm{obs}}$ passes at $0.36\sigma$; B1913 $0.01\sigma$. Bending/Mercury
2PN pieces hide below VLBI/astrometry (Fig.~69, right). The companion
toy-generator route (dense layers, $k_0 - c_2/r^2$ fits) crosses zero in
$\kappa$ and needs a $\kappa$-tweak fudge; power-law fits better on our
profiles ($R^2$ $0.91$ vs $0.81$) but the two $\kappa \to c_2$ maps disagree
($0.82$ vs $3.14$) cross-applied --- hence the open-map label. $e_{\mathrm{int}}$
weighting \emph{lowers} our $p$ ($0.94 \to 0.84$ at $0.9$, $0.78$ at $0.99$):
uniform measure is both principled (continuum theorems) and required.

\textbf{Leg-shedding kilonovae.} $M_{\mathrm{ej}} = \Delta k\,m_{\mathrm{leg}}
\times 0.1$, blue ($v \sim 0.3c$, $t_{\mathrm{peak}} \sim 0.6$--$1$\,d) + red
($v \sim 0.1c$); $L \propto t^{-1.3}$ tail. Band mags assume BC $= 0$ and
per-component peaks; POSSIS refinement queued. $g$-band verdicts robust;
$i$-band NOT claimed (one-zone $\kappa = 10$ over-traps: $t_{\mathrm{red}}
\sim 10$\,d vs observed $\sim 4$\,d decline, $m_i$ faint by $\sim 2$--$3$\,mag
--- conservative for gap-$g$ detectability). Standard $2$--$28\%$ band is a
literature EOS input, not derived.

\textbf{O5 protocol.} Trigger: $m_1 \in [2.5,5.0]\,M_\odot$, $< 200$\,Mpc,
90\% area $< 100$\,deg$^2$ (multi-detector); Rubin ToO epochs 0.5/2/5\,d in
$g+r+i+z$ (DECam/Gemini backup). Kill rule: 10 qualifying non-detections kill
BU. Archival ledger: GW230529 uninformative (single-detector, 93\% unsearched);
S250206dm unverified (check GCNs before citing). Full plan:
\texttt{docs/observation-protocol.md}. Figs.~66--69.

only the absurd $e^{2\sqrt{d-d_0}}$ escapes. Constant-$\mu$ persistence keeps
$-3$ analytically; $\mu(\chi)$ fluctuation walk gives $-1.95 \approx -2$

diffusion, carries Newton. Figs.~63--64.

(jitter, average straight), deficit $\propto \omega^2$. Bounds assume
independent edges; correlated patches of size $\xi$ relax by $\sqrt{\xi}$.
Fig.~56.

= 11.24M$; GW150914-mass ladder: healing $3.5$\,ms, scrambling $0.23$\,s,
