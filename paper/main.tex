\begin{abstract}
We formalize the intuition that a black hole interior is an almost-perfect
all:all entanglement graph. Internal edges cost no exterior space; each of the
$k$ exterior legs costs $\sim l_p^2$ of horizon area, so $A(k)=k\,l_p^2$
independent of interior node count $N$. We show (1) the complete graph $K_N$
destroys interior distance and, under finite-speed random circuits, derives the
Sekino--Susskind $t_*\sim\log N$ fast-scrambling bound; (2) mass enters via a
derived $k^*(N)=16\pi(\varepsilon N/l_p)^2$ fixed point, spin/charge consume
part of the exterior budget (extremal Kerr keeps half the legs), and the
$k\to0$ monogamy limit is a baby-universe pinch-off with an explicit nonlinear
CKW frontier; (3) micro-holes are pointlike until $k\,l_p^2 \ge 4\pi
r_{\mathrm{point}}^2$, then pop a horizon via a computed QES/island crossing,
with an LQG-style area gap. Evaporation as leg surgery reproduces the exact
Page curve (Page 1993, $-1/2$ nat correction included) with Haar-typical
fluctuations, and Hayden--Preskill mirror recovery works past $k\sim N/2$.
Simulations in \texttt{bh\_graph} accompany each claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Sec 2: Horizon area counts exterior legs}
Interior pairs $N(N{-}1)/2$ plus $k\ll N^2$ exterior legs:
\begin{equation}
A(k) = k\,l_p^2,\qquad R(k)=\sqrt{k\,l_p^2/4\pi},
\end{equation}
so $N$ drops out (Fig.~3). Schwarzschild consistency forces
$k(M)=4\pi(2M)^2/l_p^2\propto M^2$; Appendix~B derives this from MaxEnt +
self-gravity, and Appendix~H extends it to Kerr--Newman
$k_{\rm eff}(M,a,Q)=A(M,a,Q)/l_p^2$ with extremal Kerr at half the legs.
Monogamy (linear toy Fig.~4; nonlinear CKW Fig.~14) sends $e_{\rm int}\to1$,
$k\to0$: pinch-off into a baby universe.

\section{Sec 3: Micro-hole phase transition}
A point region of radius $r_{\rm point}$ embeds $k$ legs iff
$k\,l_p^2<4\pi r_{\rm point}^2$. Hence
\begin{equation}
R_{\rm obs}(k)=\begin{cases}r_{\rm point} & k\le k_{\rm crit}\\\sqrt{k\,l_p^2/4\pi} & k>k_{\rm crit}\end{cases},\quad k_{\rm crit}=4\pi r_{\rm point}^2/l_p^2,
\end{equation}
with LQG-style quantization $A\in\{0\}\cup[A_{\min},\infty)$ (Figs.~5--6).
Appendix~C shows the pop is a computed QES/island crossing at
$k_{\rm page}=S_0/(2s_{\rm leg}-l_p^2/4)$, existing iff $s_{\rm leg}>l_p^2/4$.

\appendix
\section{Finite-speed circuits: $t_*\sim\log N$ (Fig.~7)}
One gate/qubit/step; random matching (all:all) vs dimer covering (chain).
Early growth exponential on all:all; chain ballistic. Measured $N=8\ldots128$.

\section{Deriving $k(N)$ (Fig.~8)}
MaxEnt bound $k\ge Ns_{\rm node}/s_{\rm leg}$ plus $R=\sqrt{kl_p^2/4\pi}=2E$,
$E=\varepsilon N$: fixed point $k^*(N)=16\pi(\varepsilon N/l_p)^2$, stable
from any start. Predicts $\alpha(N)=k^*/N\propto N$.

\section{QES pop from entropy crossing (Fig.~9)}
$S_{\rm no}=ks_{\rm leg}$ vs $S_{\rm isl}=kl_p^2/4+\max(S_0-ks_{\rm leg},0)$;
crossing iff $s_{\rm leg}>l_p^2/4$. Explicit core+legs min-cut agrees.

\section{Page curve from leg surgery (Fig.~10)}
$S_{\rm rad}(t)=\min(t,N_{\rm eff}-t)$; area identical for wiring-only vs
standard evaporation: $A$ tracks $k$ alone.

\section{QEC mirror (Fig.~11)}
$err(k)=\min(1/2,2^{N/2+1-k})$; 99\% threshold
$k\ge N/2+1+\log_2 100$; $k\to0$ seals information off.

\section{Robustness (Fig.~11)}
Log slope $1/\log_2(1+p)$ at all $p$; exact quadraticity at all $\varepsilon$;
sharp QES boundary at $s_{\rm leg}=l_p^2/4$.

\section{Kerr--Newman (Fig.~12)}
$r_+=M+\sqrt{M^2-a^2-Q^2}$, $A=4\pi(r_+^2+a^2)$; spin budget
$1-A(M,a)/A(M,0)\to1/2$ at extremality. Area law survives.

\section{Exact Page curve (Fig.~13)}
$S(m,n)=H(mn)-H(n)-(m-1)/2n$ nats; $-1/2$ nat dip at turnover; Haar samples
($N=8$) track the mean: typical, not tuned.

\section{Nonlinear monogamy (Fig.~14)}
Family $\cos t|\Phi^+\rangle|0\rangle+\sin t|00\rangle|1\rangle$: Wootters
$C^2_{AB}$ vs $\tau_{E|AB}$ frontier lies strictly below $x+y=1$ (e.g.\ 0.70
at $t=0.3$); CKW $\ge0$ everywhere; baby universe at $(1,0)$.

\section{OTOC + phenomenology (Fig.~15)}
$C(t)\sim e^{\lambda t}/N$ on all:all gives $t^*=\log N/\lambda$ vs ballistic
$N/v$ on chains. PBHs evaporating today $\sim10^{18}$--$10^{19}$ Planck masses;
sub-critical holes evaporate without greybody suppression; echo toy
$\Delta t\sim M\log k$; nearest-term test is analogue scrambling on
programmable processors.

\section{Tensor network derives $\varepsilon$ (Fig.~16)}
Random star TN verifies $S_{\rm bdy}=\min(N\log d,k\log D)$; minimal wiring
$k_{\min}=S_{\rm bulk}/\log D$. Two routes fix $\varepsilon$: QES coincidence
$\varepsilon=(l_p/N)\sqrt{k_{\rm page}/16\pi}$ and crossover
$\varepsilon=l_p\sqrt{s_{\rm node}/(16\pi\log D\,N_{\rm match})}$.
