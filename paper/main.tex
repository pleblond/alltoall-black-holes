\begin{abstract}
We formalize the intuition that a black hole interior is an almost-perfect
all:all entanglement graph. Internal edges cost no exterior space; each of the
$k$ exterior legs costs $\sim l_p^2$ of horizon area, so $A(k)=k\,l_p^2$
independent of interior node count $N$. We show (1) the complete graph $K_N$
destroys interior distance and, under finite-speed random circuits, derives the
Sekino--Susskind $t_*\sim\log N$ fast-scrambling bound; (2) mass enters via a
derived $k^*(N)=16\pi(\varepsilon N/l_p)^2$ fixed point, spin/charge consume
part of the exterior budget (extremal Kerr keeps half the legs), and the
$k\to0$ monogamy limit is a baby-universe pinch-off with an explicit nonlinear
CKW frontier; (3) micro-holes are pointlike until $k\,l_p^2 \ge 4\pi
r_{\mathrm{point}}^2$, then pop a horizon via a computed QES/island crossing,
with an LQG-style area gap. Evaporation as leg surgery reproduces the exact
Page curve (Page 1993, $-1/2$ nat correction included) with Haar-typical
fluctuations, and Hayden--Preskill mirror recovery works past $k\sim N/2$.
Simulations in \texttt{bh\_graph} accompany each claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Sec 1: All:all = no interior space}
Interior wiring $G_N$ compared across $K_N$, chain, grid, random regular under

cover time~1 at every $N$ (Figs.~1--2). Finite-speed random-matching circuits
(Appendix~A) promote this to a derived $t_*\approx\log_2 N/\log_2(1+p)$.

\section{Sec 2: Horizon area counts exterior legs}
Interior pairs $N(N{-}1)/2$ plus $k\ll N^2$ exterior legs:
\begin{equation}
A(k) = k\,l_p^2,\qquad R(k)=\sqrt{k\,l_p^2/4\pi},
\end{equation}
so $N$ drops out (Fig.~3). Schwarzschild consistency forces
$k(M)=4\pi(2M)^2/l_p^2\propto M^2$; Appendix~B derives this from MaxEnt +
self-gravity, and Appendix~H extends it to Kerr--Newman
$k_{\rm eff}(M,a,Q)=A(M,a,Q)/l_p^2$ with extremal Kerr at half the legs.
Monogamy (linear toy Fig.~4; nonlinear CKW Fig.~14) sends $e_{\rm int}\to1$,
$k\to0$: pinch-off into a baby universe.

\section{Sec 3: Micro-hole phase transition}
A point region of radius $r_{\rm point}$ embeds $k$ legs iff
$k\,l_p^2<4\pi r_{\rm point}^2$. Hence
\begin{equation}
R_{\rm obs}(k)=\begin{cases}r_{\rm point} & k\le k_{\rm crit}\\\sqrt{k\,l_p^2/4\pi} & k>k_{\rm crit}\end{cases},\quad k_{\rm crit}=4\pi r_{\rm point}^2/l_p^2,
\end{equation}
with LQG-style quantization $A\in\{0\}\cup[A_{\min},\infty)$ (Figs.~5--6).
Appendix~C shows the pop is a computed QES/island crossing at
$k_{\rm page}=S_0/(2s_{\rm leg}-l_p^2/4)$, existing iff $s_{\rm leg}>l_p^2/4$.

\section{Discussion}
