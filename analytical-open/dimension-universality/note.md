# Dimension can be universal while interference is not

SCALE-0 banks \(d_H\to 2\) with J2 and the square control equal to machine
precision. That is a statement about the quadratic symbol. On J2,

\[
\varepsilon=-8+2|q|^2+O(q^4),
\]

and the square quotient has the same Hessian \(4I\). Any substrate whose
long-wave Hamiltonian is a multiple of the Laplacian shares this volume
exponent. Universality of \(d_H\) is the universality of that quadratic
form.

F-interference, which SUBCLASS0 proved is not a function of the frozen
spectral feature vector, probes the propagator at the slit separation.
Relative to the quadratic term the first correction is \(O(q^2)=O((a/r)^2)\).
At distances of a few cells the quartic and the plaquette holonomy are
order-one. Two graphs with the same Hessian and different
\(\gamma/\beta\) (see `lorentz-emergent/note.md`) agree on \(d_H\) and
disagree on fringes.

**Consequence.** A test that promotes "same dimension" to "same universality
class" for interference, tunnelling, or angular wavefronts is using the
wrong invariant. The invariant that has already split is
\(\gamma/\beta\) together with the plaquette. Dimension universality, as
earned, should be left at the volume exponent. Extending it to F is a new
claim and the scale estimate says it fails at \(r=O(1)\), which is where
those gates operate.
