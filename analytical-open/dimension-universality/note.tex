\section{Quadratic form against a fringe}
\label{sec:h-dimuniv}

SCALE-0 identifies the volume exponent of \(J_2\) with that of the square,
to machine precision, out to large \(L\).
Both have Hessian \(4I\) at the band bottom.
Any long-wave generator that is a multiple of the Laplacian shares that
exponent.
The relative size of the first correction is \(O(q^2)=O((a/r)^2)\).
At distances of a few cells the quartic ratio \(\gamma/\beta\) and the
plaquette holonomy are order one.
Theorem~\ref{thm:ratio} says that ratio is zero on the separable lattices
and \(2\) on a rotationally invariant symbol.
Two graphs can therefore share \(d_H\) and differ at the slit.
That split is consistent with a feature vector that classifies the volume
exponent and does not classify interference.
