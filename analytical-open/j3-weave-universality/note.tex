\section{Quadratic match against a lifted zero}
\label{sec:h-j3w}

The \(J_3\) Hessian at the band bottom is \(4I_3\).
A chain of \(J_2\) sheets with transverse scale \(t_\perp\) has
\(\mathrm{Hess}=\mathrm{diag}(4,4,t_\perp)\).
The coordinate change \(z'=z\sqrt{t_\perp/4}\) identifies the quadratic forms
and leaves the quartic ratio of Theorem~\ref{thm:ratio} equal to \(0\) in the
plane, with a different transverse quartic.
The \(J_3\) flat band is the zero eigenvalue of
\(\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}\) at every crystal momentum.
A stitch with a nonzero matrix element between the sheet-symmetric and
sheet-antisymmetric vectors lifts the corresponding zero.
At small stitch amplitude the splitting is linear for a coherent stack and
of the order of the transverse bandwidth for a disordered weave.
A disjoint union of \(S\) sheets starts with \(S L^2\) flat zeros.
After the stitches those zeros are no longer the \(J_3\) count
\(L^3\) plus touchings.
Section~\ref{sec:ell} is the metric form of the same \(t_\perp\), read as
\(\ell_W\) rather than as a hopping.
