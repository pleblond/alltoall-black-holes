# Rewire has no amplitude to test until a measure exists

REWIRE0-DEGENERATE: on the tested states, every exact principle in the
frozen list either ties or is absent, out to L28. A degree-preserving
rewire changes the local isomorphism type at

\[
\Delta E=-2(B_{\mathrm{add}}-B_{\mathrm{remove}}).
\]

When the two re-pairings of a 4-cycle have the same \(\Delta E\), the bond
does not select. Symmetry, energy, current, and the ledger all fail to
break the tie. That is an earned no-go, not a small rate.

A weak-interaction reading needs branching ratios. Branching ratios are a
measure on the tie. MEASURE0 and FIBER0 say no such measure is forced.
Computing a Fermi constant, a lifetime, or a preferred channel from present
data would be a fit to a flat direction.

**What is still a real test.** The degeneracy itself, on graphs that are
not J2: if a 3D substrate or a weave stitch lifts the tie by an energy
splitting \(\Delta\) that stays finite as \(L\) grows, the no-go was
substrate-specific and a rate might later be \(\Delta\) over a derived
attempt frequency. If the splitting closes, the weak-channel identification
has no small parameter. That splitting is a single diagonalization of two
rewired graphs. It is not a Monte Carlo of outcomes.
