\section{Two speeds from one separable symbol}
\label{sec:h-radial}

For \(\varepsilon=-4\sum_{i=1}^d\cos q_i\) the maxima of group velocity satisfy
\[
\frac{v_E^{\max}}{v_M}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}.
\]
The Manhattan value is the causal bound of a sum of axis hops
(\(8\) on \(J_2\), \(12\) on \(J_3\)).
Near \(q=0\), \(v=4q+O(q^3)\) is parallel to \(q\) up to the deflection in
Theorem~\ref{thm:ratio}.
A localized source, superposed, fills a Euclidean ball at the peak and a
diamond at the support of the forerunner.
The ratio \(1/\sqrt{d}\) is the relation between those two speeds.
It is the same separability that forces \(\gamma=0\) in Section~\ref{sec:quartic}.
