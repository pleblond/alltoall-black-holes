\section{Ball growth at fixed field}
\label{sec:h-frust}

For two graphs on the same vertex set and one fixed \(\psi\),
\[
E_\psi(G')-E_\psi(G)
=-2\sum_{e\in E'\setminus E}B_e
+2\sum_{e\in E\setminus E'}B_e.
\]
No term in \(H=-A\) depends on \(d_{\mathrm{eff}}\) except through the edge set.
If every deleted stitch has \(B_e=0\), the field energy is unchanged and the
ball growth may still change.
At that locus a dimension and the energy of Section~\ref{sec:h-mass} separate.
The bond \(B_e=0\) is the same point as \(P_-=1/2\) in Lemma~\ref{lem:bond-expand}.
A frustration energy proportional to \(|d_{\mathrm{local}}-3|\) would be a
reparameterization of the bond sum, and only where the deleted edges happen
to carry \(B_e\) correlated with that difference.
The identity above does not force the correlation.
