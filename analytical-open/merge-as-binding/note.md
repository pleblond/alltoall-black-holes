\[
\sum_i R_i=-(E^{\mathrm{final}}_{\mathrm{known}}-E^{\mathrm{initial}}_{\mathrm{known}}),
\]

because between events \(E_\psi\) is conserved by unitarity. If radiation
leaves the region during formation, it is part of the final known energy

the path differed and radiation differed. Identifying \(\sum R\) with the
mass double-counts path data that the spectrum has already forgotten.
