\section{Level dependence of a Rice rate}
\label{sec:h-hazard}

For a stationary process \(X(t)=B_{ij}(t)-\mu\) with variance \(m_0\) and
derivative variance \(m_2\), the mean upcrossing rate of a level \(B_*\) is
\[
\lambda(B_*)
=\frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{m_2}{m_0}}
\exp\Bigl(-\frac{(B_*-\mu)^2}{2m_0}\Bigr).
\]
Both moments are integrals against the spectral measure of \(B_{ij}(t)\).
The family \(\lambda(B_*)\), not a single rate, is the content of the formula.
A two-mode parent replaces the exponential by a sum of deltas at the
arccosine roots.
A single Haar vector determines the law of the snapshot \(B/q\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:haar}) and does not determine \(m_2\).
The exact codimension-\(2\) condition of Lemma~\ref{lem:mu} has \(\lambda=0\).
The unfixed ratios inside \(B_*\) are the same debt as in the conditional
conservation identity.
A rate independent of the level would lie outside this formula.
