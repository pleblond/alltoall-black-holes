\section{\texorpdfstring{Three clocks already fixed by \(H\)}{Three clocks already fixed by H}}
\label{sec:h-life}

Section~\ref{sec:codim} separates three waiting laws that do not require a
new measure.
The radiative width is \(\Gamma=2\pi|V|^2\rho(E)\) with \(\rho\) from
\eqref{eq:dos}.
The bond threshold is the arccosine comb of Section~\ref{sec:h-timing},
which on a few-mode parent is a train of steps.
The exact codimension-\(2\) jet condition is missed, so its structural rate
is zero.
A constant hazard appears in the further approximation that \(B(t)-B_*\) is a
mixing Gaussian process and events are upcrossings.
That rate is the Rice formula in Section~\ref{sec:h-hazard}, with moments
taken from the spectral measure of the same edge process.
The parent that is spectrally bound and carries a stored \(\xi\) is the case
in which the structural clock can be the replay of Section~\ref{sec:h-daughters}
rather than the radiative width.
