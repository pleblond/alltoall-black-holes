\section{A drop of the reported exponent}
\label{sec:h-bb}

Section~\ref{sec:h-soup} identifies a geometric phase with a percolation
of \(4\)-cycles at fixed degree.
On the tree side of that transition the ball grows exponentially, and a
local logarithmic derivative \(d\ln V/d\ln r\) is large.
On the sheet side it equals \(2\).
Formation of geometry, in this order parameter, is a decrease of the
reported exponent toward \(2\), followed by a weave lift toward a window
at \(3\) if stitches percolate in a one-dimensional meeting complex
\eqref{eq:deff}.

A path on which \(d_{\mathrm{eff}}\) rises from \(1\) toward \(3\) is a
different curve in the \((f_\square,d_{\mathrm{eff}})\) plane
(Section~\ref{sec:soup}).
No earned firing law produces \(f_\square(t)\).
The static family parameterized by cycle density is the phase diagram.
A dynamical time is a later parameterization of the same axis, and only
if some rule changes \(f_\square\).
The width of the window at \(3\), when it appears, remains \(S\ell_W\).
