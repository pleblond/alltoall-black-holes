\section{\texorpdfstring{Unitary width and a replay of \(\xi\)}{Unitary width and a replay of xi}}
\label{sec:h-splitdecay}

An eigenvalue inside the essential spectrum couples to the continuum
\eqref{eq:dos}.
The golden-rule width is \(\Gamma=2\pi|V|^2\rho(E)\).
On the \(J_2\) edge, \(\rho\) tends to a positive constant, so the width does
not shut off as the resonance approaches \(-8\).
On the \(J_3\) edge, \(\rho\) vanishes as a square root.
This evolution is \(e^{-itH}\).
It does not read \(Q\).

A sum-map split that consumes a stored \(\xi\) returns the STORE preimage.
A split that creates \(\xi\) is Section~\ref{sec:h-split}, and
Lemma~\ref{lem:mu} removes every cover that fails \eqref{eq:mudot},
independently of \(d\).
A motif with no eigenvalue below the edge
(Proposition~\ref{prop:clique} and the remark after it)
has the unitary clock available and has no spectral gap to protect a
later structural split.
