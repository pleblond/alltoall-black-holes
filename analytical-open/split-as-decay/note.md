# Two decay channels, and only one of them is a split

**Radiative.** An eigenvalue inside the essential spectrum couples to the
continuum. The width is \(\Gamma=2\pi|V|^2\rho(E)\) in the golden-rule
approximation, with \(\rho\) from `why-three-dimensions/note.md`. The
evolution is the earned unitary \(e^{-itH}\). Products are continuum waves
of the same field. No \(Q\) entry is required, and the time dependence is
deterministic given \(\psi(0)\). On J2, \(\rho\) at the edge is a positive
constant, so there is no long-lived shallow resonance. On J3, \(\rho\)
vanishes at the edge and shallow widths are small.

**Structural.** A sum-map split consuming a stored \(\xi=(c,d)\) returns
the unique preimage (STORE0). That is deterministic in the daughters. A
split that must **create** \(\xi\) is the open fiber. The jet calculation
in `dynamical-jet-admissibility/note.md` says a generic created \(\xi\)
fails first-order continuity of the summed amplitude, while a stored
\(\xi\) passes all orders because the recovered state is the true
predecessor.

A motif with no eigenvalue below the band edge decays radiatively on the
unitary timescale even if its \(Q\) is empty. Looking for a stochastic
structural lifetime on that motif will not see one; the packet has already
left by radiation. The split test is reserved for states that are spectral
bound states **and** carry a store entry. Those are the composites of
`matter-stable-composite/note.md` that were formed by merges.
