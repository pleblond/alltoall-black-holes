\section{Vanishing edge density against a weave count}
\label{sec:h-why3}

The density \eqref{eq:dos} vanishes at a nondegenerate band edge if and only
if \(d\ge 3\).
The weave reading \(d_{\mathrm{obs}}=2+d_M\) equals \(3\) when the meeting
complex is one-dimensional.
These are independent.
The first forbids a finite golden-rule width at threshold
(Section~\ref{sec:h-splitdecay}).
The second is minimality among polynomial meeting graphs, and it uses the
imported Maxwell dimension \(2\).
Neither statement is implied by the other.
A meeting dimension \(d_M\ge 2\) gives \(d_{\mathrm{obs}}\ge 4\), which still
has a vanishing edge density.
The clique gap of Proposition~\ref{prop:clique} can be formed in any of
these dimensions; the width of whatever remains inside the band cannot.
