\section{A pure power against a window}
\label{sec:h-fixedd}

For \(V(r)=A(r/\ell)^d\) with \(A\), \(d\), and \(\ell\) independent of
\(r\),
\[
d_{\mathrm{eff}}=d,\qquad
\frac{\partial d_{\mathrm{eff}}}{\partial\ell}=0.
\]
Changing \(\ell\) at fixed \(d\) is a pure rescaling.
On a graph whose every edge has length \(1\), arrival times in hopping
units scale with the distances.
The ratio of a graph clock to a graph ruler stays zero.

The ratio becomes visible when one clock does not scale.
The gap \(m\) of Section~\ref{sec:h-mass} is set by local coordination.
If those local edges are held fixed while \(\ell_W\) grows, \(m\) stays
constant in hopping units and \(\ell_W\) grows in the same units.
The window exponent \(3\) is unchanged for a probe that remains inside
\[
\ell_W\ll r_{\mathrm{probe}}\ll S\ell_W.
\]
A probe at a fixed number of cells eventually leaves that window as
\(\ell_W\) grows, and the local reading returns to \(2\).
The inequality is the condition under which \(d_{\mathrm{eff}}\) and a
growing transverse scale are compatible.
On the woven ladder \(\ell_W\ge 1.77\), so the window, measured in units
of \(\ell_W\), is the finite range up to \(S\).
