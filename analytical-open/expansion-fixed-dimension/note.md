# Dimension and scale factor are independent only inside a pure power

For \(V(r)=A(r/\ell)^d\) with \(A,d,\ell\) independent of \(r\),

\[
d_{\mathrm{eff}}=d,\qquad\frac{\partial d_{\mathrm{eff}}}{\partial\ell}=0.
\]

Changing \(\ell\) at fixed \(d\) is a pure rescaling. On a graph whose
every edge has length \(1\), that rescaling is invisible to every
graph-intrinsic clock: arrival times, in hopping units, scale the same way
as distances. The ratio that cosmology calls \(H\), measured by a graph
clock against a graph ruler, stays zero.

The ratio becomes observable when one clock does **not** scale. The
spectral gap \(m\) of a tense motif (`mass-structural-distortion/note.md`)
is set by local coordination. If local edges are held fixed while
\(\ell_W\) grows, \(m\) stays constant in hopping units and \(\ell_W\)
grows in the same units. That is expansion at fixed local physics, and it
is also expansion at fixed local dimension \(2\), with the window exponent
\(3\) unchanged as long as the probe remains inside
\(\ell_W\ll r\ll S\ell_W\).

If the probe is a fixed number of cells, growing \(\ell_W\) eventually
pushes the probe out of the window into the local \(2\)D regime. "Expansion
at fixed dimension" holds only while the probe's graph radius stays inside
the window. The inequality \(\ell_W(t)\ll r_{\mathrm{probe}}\ll S\ell_W(t)\)
is the condition to publish beside any claim that \(d_{\mathrm{eff}}\)
stayed at \(3\) while \(a\) grew. On the WOVEN ladder the window is narrow
in units of \(\ell_W\), and a few e-foldings of \(\ell_W\) close it.
