\section{\texorpdfstring{What of \(\xi=(c,d)\) is already fixed by \(B\)}{What of xi=(c,d) is already fixed by B}}
\label{sec:h-split}

A daughter that is not a stored replay has fibre coordinate \(d\in\C\) and a
discrete cover.
The combination that enters Remark~\ref{rem:h2} is only the real number
\(P_-=1/2-B/q\).
The phase of \(d\) at \(B=0\) is the current and is invisible to \(P_-\).
So the fibre splits into the cover, the bond, and the current.

A stored entry fixes all three (STORE).
A Haar pair, with nothing stored, has \(P_-\) uniform on \([0,1]\)
by Theorem~\ref{thm:haar}, with no free parameter in that marginal.
A scale-separated pair has \(P_-\to 1/2\) by Proposition~\ref{prop:lambda},
and the phase remains free unless the current or the jet fixes it.
The \(520\)-parameter fibre measure is a law on the whole fibre compatible
with every earned constraint.
The marginal of \(P_-\) under a Haar draw is narrower than that, and it is
already computed.
Lemma~\ref{lem:mu} constrains which covers can meet \(C^1\) continuity at all.
