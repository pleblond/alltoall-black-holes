\section{No drift of an orientation matrix}
\label{sec:h-orient}

The chain weave has no ambient normals, so
\(M_{ab}=\langle n_a n_b\rangle\) is not an observable of that ensemble
(Section~\ref{sec:h-iso}).
On an ensemble that does assign normals, the orientation of a sheet is a
function of \(G\).
Between events \(G\) is fixed, so \(M_{ab}\) is fixed.
Degree-preserving re-pairings that could rotate a local frame are the
tied moves of REWIRE0: no bond, current, or energy drift selects a
rotation (Section~\ref{sec:codim}).
The deterministic drift of \(M_{ab}\) is zero.
A diffusion in orientation space would be a measure on those ties.

Under every earned deterministic rule the eigenvalue gap of \(M_{ab}\) is
therefore constant in time.
Isotropy, when it is present, is a property of the initial ensemble or of
the construction.
Haar measure on orientations gives \(M_{ab}=I/3\) by symmetry.
The Hessian of \(J_3\) is \(4I\) by the form of \(\varepsilon_{J_3}\).
Neither is an attractor of \(H=-A\).
A declining gap of \(M_{ab}\) along a trajectory generated by \(H\) would
mean the angle update was not that Hamiltonian.
