# Daughters are fixed if and only if the consumed entry is fixed

STORE0: given \(\xi=(c,d)\) and the merged state, the preimage is unique,
including the endpoint-swap gauge \(d\to -d\). Q-DYN-0b: \(Q\) does not
change between events, so the entry available at the split is the entry
written at the merge.

If the composite holds several entries, "consume \(Q\)" still leaves the
discrete choice of which entry. That choice is deterministic if at most
one entry satisfies the jet obstruction of
`dynamical-jet-admissibility/note.md`. It is stochastic only if two
entries both satisfy it and nothing selects between them. The continuous
coordinate inside a single entry is not the stochastic piece; the entry
already contains it.

If the parent has an empty store, FIBER0-DEBT applies and the daughters
are not fixed by earned physics. `split-outcome-stochasticity/note.md`
gives the Haar marginal in that case, which is a parameter-free baseline,
not a resolution of the debt. Report daughters against that baseline
before introducing a new measure. A statistically significant departure
from uniform \(P_-\), from the arcsine law, and from a delta at \(1/2\) is
the signal that a selection law is operating. Agreement with one of the
three is not a new law.
