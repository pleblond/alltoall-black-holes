\section{Daughters from a consumed entry}
\label{sec:h-daughters}

Given \(\xi=(c,d)\) and the merged state, the sum-map preimage is unique up
to the swap \(d\mapsto -d\).
Between events the entry does not change, so the entry present at a split is
the entry written at the merge.
Several entries leave the discrete choice of which entry.
Lemma~\ref{lem:mu} admits at most those entries whose cover satisfies
\(s+\sum_C\psi=0\); an asymmetric survivor fixes at most one \(d\).
An empty store returns the situation of Section~\ref{sec:h-split}.
The three parameter-free marginals on \(P_-\) are the Haar law, the arcsine
law, and a point mass at \(1/2\).
