# Infrared dimension 3 needs a noncompact meeting direction

From `weave-random-sheet-3d/note.md`: a ring of finitely many sheets is
three-dimensional only in a window and is a thick two-dimensional stack in
the infrared. Macroscopic 3D as a stable phase of locally 2D sheets
requires one of the following.

- An infinite line of sheets, \(S=\infty\), with \(\ell_W\) finite. Then
  \(V\sim r^3/\ell_W\) for every \(r\gg\ell_W\). The third direction is
  noncompact. The metric remains anisotropic by \(\ell_W\)
  (`disorder-improves-isotropy/note.md`).
- A meeting complex that is itself a noncompact 3-dimensional foam of
  intersections, so that no axis stays at a parametrically different cost.
  That is not the chain construction.

A campaign on \((S,L)=(16,24)\) cannot see a stable infrared 3. It can see
the window. Calling a window result "macroscopic 3D" overstates the
construction by the compactification radius \(S\ell_W\). The honest
extrapolation to a cosmological volume is the infinite-line formula, which
still carries the axis ratio \(\ell_W\), plus the isotropy obstruction
recorded in `dark-energy-weave/note.md`.
