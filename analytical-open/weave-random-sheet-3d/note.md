# Volume exponent 3 is an intermediate window, and it is anisotropic

The chain-meeting count from the WEAVE-0 preregistration is the starting
point. Sheets of intrinsic area growth \(r^2\), meeting graph of dimension
\(d_M\), crossover \(\ell_W\):

\[
V(r)\sim
\begin{cases}
r^2 & r\ll\ell_W,\\
(r/\ell_W)^{d_M}\,r^2 & \ell_W\ll r\ll R_\perp,\\
S\,r^2 & r\gg R_\perp,
\end{cases}
\]

with \(R_\perp=S\ell_W\) when \(d_M=1\) and the meeting graph is a ring of
\(S\) sheets. For that ring, \(d_M=1\) and the middle regime is
\(V\sim r^3/\ell_W\).

**Theorem.** On an infinite line of sheets (\(S=\infty\)) the middle regime
is the infrared: \(V\sim r^3/\ell_W\) for all \(r\gg\ell_W\). The return to
thick two-dimensional growth is the finite-\(S\) compactification of the
third direction, circumference \(S\ell_W\). A finite-ring campaign that
reads \(3\) only inside a window and \(2\) outside has confirmed the
mechanism. A finite-ring campaign that never sees the re-entrant tail has
not yet reached \(r\sim S\ell_W\); that absence is not evidence of a stable
infrared \(3\).

The metric inside the window is the anisotropic one in
`disorder-improves-isotropy/note.md`. Report \(d_{\mathrm{eff}}\) and the
axis ratio together. A single number \(d_{\mathrm{eff}}\approx 3\) does not
identify the chain weave with J3 (see `j3-weave-universality/note.md`).

Dense meeting (\(d_M=\infty\)) has no middle regime: \(V\sim S r^2\) as
soon as stitches are felt. A 3-regular random meeting graph is locally a
tree, volume exponential in graph distance, and has no stable power \(3\).
Those two discriminators are already in the preregistration. The new
reporting requirement is the axis ratio on the chain, which the volume gate
does not see.
