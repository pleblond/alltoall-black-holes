# \(Q\) does not gravitate between events

Q-DYN-0b: \(Q\) is constant on every waiting rung, and \(R(M,Q)\) is
recomputed when a structural map runs. It is not a term in \(H\). The
linear carrier does not see \(Q\). Hidden-sector differences at fixed
\((G,\psi)\) change \(B\) only when they change \(\psi\); a pure store
difference at fixed \(\psi\) changes neither \(B\) nor the arrival metric.

A store entry affects geometry only at the event that reads it, through
the graph the split or merge writes. Between events

\[
\frac{\partial H}{\partial Q}=0.
\]

There is no secular \(\delta G\) sourced by a frozen \(Q\). The ledger
sentence "disturbances flow into \(Q/G\)" can describe the bookkeeping at
an event (\(d\) moves into \(Q\), \(R\) accounts the energy jump). It does
not describe a force during the waiting interval.

The test that matches the theorem is a null: two states with the same
\((G,\psi)\) and different \(Q\) must show identical \(\psi(t)\), identical
\(B(t)\), and identical arrival times until an event is allowed to read
\(Q\). Q-DYN-0b already ran the frozen-\(Q\) half of this on its battery.
Extending it to a gravitational observable (a far \(\delta B\), a deflection)
without an event in between should remain a null. A positive far signal at
fixed \(G\) would contradict \(\partial H/\partial Q=0\) and would mean the
implementation updated the graph or the field from \(Q\) outside an event.
