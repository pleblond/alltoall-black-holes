\section{\texorpdfstring{A store that does not enter \(H\)}{A store that does not enter H}}
\label{sec:h-gravq}

The account \(R\) is recomputed when a structural map runs.
It is not a term in \(H\).
The linear carrier does not see \(Q\).
A pure store difference at fixed \(\psi\) changes neither \(B\) nor the
arrival metric.
Between events
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:dHdQ}
\frac{\partial H}{\partial Q}=0.
\end{equation}
There is no secular \(\delta G\) sourced by a frozen \(Q\).

The sentence that disturbances flow into \(Q\) and \(G\) can describe the
bookkeeping at an event: \(d\) moves into the store, and \(R\) accounts the
energy jump (Section~\ref{sec:dos}).
It does not describe a force during the waiting interval.

\(\psi(t)\), identical \(B(t)\), and identical arrival times until an event
is allowed to read \(Q\).
A far signal at fixed \(G\) would contradict \eqref{eq:dHdQ}.
Section~\ref{sec:kernel} counts the dimension of the store that this
derivative does not see.
What is filed at the event and then unseen by \(H\) includes that complex
line together with the discrete orbit of Proposition~\ref{prop:covers}.
